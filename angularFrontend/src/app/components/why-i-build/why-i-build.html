Why I built BTG Spanish

I took Spanish throughout elementary school, high school, and even college. But when I found myself living with a host family in the middle of a pueblo in Oaxaca, Mexico — I could not communicate at all.

Thank goodness I was there to attend an immersive Spanish school, where I learned to communicate by practicing in real time with native speakers every single day for six months.

Years after I moved back home, I noticed something unsettling. I was slipping back into the former version of myself — the one who existed before Mexico. Getting nervous to speak. Forgetting everything the moment I had an opportunity to actually use it.

I looked around at friends who had also learned Spanish in school and realized they were in the same boat. It wasn't that I was smarter or had studied harder. The difference maker was time spent in actual, real conversations.

So I built the bridge from knowing Spanish to speaking it.

Even our beginners are matched with a native speaker for regular conversation practice from day one — because the best way to learn, even at the very beginning, is to create opportunities to speak.

BTG Spanish has grown into a robust program supporting adult Spanish learners at every stage. I spend most of my time in the community — supporting our learners directly and creating opportunities for them to grow into confident Spanish speakers.

And the best part? I get to witness the full circle moment that so many of them experience as they step into the confident Spanish they've always dreamed of having.
